Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 Build: Version 1.2.0 (Alpha) Location: Clinton, CT 06413

Overview

The Morgan Newsletter is a non-commercial school application built for The Morgan School in Clinton, Connecticut. We collect only what is strictly necessary to authenticate accounts, display school journalism, and report sports scores. We do not sell personal data, track users across third-party apps, or display commercial advertisements. Passwords are cryptographically hashed, and users have the self-service ability to edit their profile or permanently delete their account at any time.

1. Privacy Commitment

The Morgan School and the Morgan Research Club are committed to protecting the privacy, security, and digital safety of students, faculty, staff, alumni, and Clinton residents. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data practices regarding collection, storage, operational use, and deletion rights within the Morgan Newsletter mobile application (iOS & Android).

2. Information We Collect

We collect only information required to provide an authentic, safe, and functional school newsletter and athletic reporting experience.

Category Data Elements Operational Purpose
Account Information Display name, email address (personal or school), cryptographically hashed password. Account creation, sign-in verification, password resets, and critical policy communications.
Profile Details Optional profile avatar photo and optional biography text. Personalizing user profile presence within the school community.
User-Generated Content Articles, text posts, comments, photos, video highlights, and athletic scores. Publishing school journalism and sports coverage (limited to authorized contributors).
Interactions & Social Likes given, followed student/team profiles, and in-app notifications. Delivering relevant updates, followers feeds, and engagement alerts.
Feedback & Access Requests Bug reproduction details, feature suggestions, access request reasons. Reviewing privilege elevation requests and troubleshooting app bugs.
Device & Notification Tokens Expo Push Notification Token, operating system type (iOS / Android). Delivering timely push alerts for sports scores, game results, and announcements (can be disabled anytime).

Device Permissions Requested:

  • Photo Library & Camera (iOS NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription & Android permissions): The application only requests access to your photo library when you affirmatively select a profile picture or choose to attach media to an authorized article or post. Photos are never accessed without your direct action.
  • Push Notifications: Requested at runtime to deliver alerts for athletic scores, game schedules, and announcements. Users can disable notifications at any time via device settings.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

Unlike commercial platforms, our non-profit educational focus ensures we do not collect intrusive personal data:

  • No Continuous GPS Tracking: We do not track or log physical GPS location coordinates.
  • No Third-Party Advertising Trackers: We do not integrate advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking identifiers (IDFA/GAID), or commercial behavioral tracking.
  • No Financial Information: The application is 100% free with no in-app purchases or billing details collected.
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, rent, or trade student, faculty, or community data to commercial brokers or advertisers.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for school operational and communication purposes:

  1. Authentication & Security: To verify user identity, encrypt sign-in sessions, prevent spam accounts, and handle password resets.
  2. Content Delivery: To curate and broadcast athletic scores, student reporting, and event schedules.
  3. Administrative Communication: To dispatch password reset codes, security notices, and terms/policy updates.
  4. Push Alerts: To notify users of new scorecards, comments, or followers when opted-in.
  5. Community Safety: To ensure all submissions comply with school standards and maintain a safe digital environment for minors.
  6. Technical Maintenance: To diagnose errors submitted via the in-app "Report A Bug/Feature" tool.

5. Cloud Storage, Encryption & Security

We employ industry-standard security safeguards designed to protect community information:

  • Cryptographically Hashed Passwords: Passwords are never stored in plaintext. They are encrypted using one-way cryptographic hashing algorithms (bcrypt/argon2 via Supabase GoTrue Auth) on server infrastructure.
  • PostgreSQL Database: Records reside within PostgreSQL databases managed by Supabase and hosted within enterprise-grade Amazon Web Services (AWS) data centers.
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the mobile application and backend services is protected with Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security (SSL/TLS).
  • Local Device Storage: Session tokens are saved locally in encrypted storage (AsyncStorage) to maintain secure sessions.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on reputable technical service providers to maintain backend infrastructure:

  • Supabase Inc.: Provides database hosting (PostgreSQL), user authentication, serverless Edge Functions, and backend API infrastructure.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Provides physical cloud hosting and data center infrastructure hosting the Supabase clusters.
  • Expo / EAS (650 Industries): Powers push notification delivery and the React Native mobile application framework.

7. Student & Minor Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because The Morgan School educates students of all high school grade levels and serves Clinton, CT families, minor safety is our primary focus.

No Minimum Age for Safe Browsing: There is no age restriction to view the application because every public article, post, and score card is actively moderated and filtered by school faculty. The environment is kept strictly school-appropriate for all ages.

COPPA Compliance: The Morgan Newsletter complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not collect student personal information for commercial profiling. Accounts created by minors must use valid school credentials or be authorized by parents/guardians.

Parental Rights: Parents and legal guardians may review, request deletion of, or refuse further collection of their child’s personal data by contacting our administrative team at Hussainisyed2009@gmail.com.

8. User Rights & Self-Service Account Deletion

Users maintain full control over their account data:

  • Profile Editing: Users may edit display names, biographies, and avatar photos at any time in Profile > Edit Profile.
  • Push Notifications: Push alerts can be toggled on or off at any moment in iOS or Android system settings.
  • Self-Service Account Deletion: Any user can delete their account at any time directly in the app under Settings > Delete Account. This immediately invokes a secure serverless Edge Function (delete-user) to permanently delete account credentials and profile records.
  • Data Inquiries: Users may request a summary of data linked to their email by contacting support.

9. Policy Modifications & Notifications

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or statutory requirements.

Notification Procedure: When material modifications occur, registered users will be notified via:

  1. A notice post or announcement banner displayed within the application; and
  2. An email notice sent to registered email addresses on file.

Continued use of the application following the publication of revised Privacy Policy terms constitutes acceptance of the changes.
